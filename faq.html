Spray foam & insulation FAQ for Calgary

Practical answers for homeowners and property managers comparing spray foam and insulation contractors in Calgary and Southern Alberta. Last updated November 10, 2026.

Closed-cell spray foam is denser, adds structural rigidity, resists moisture, and gives a higher R-value per inch, which suits rim joists, basements, and exterior applications. Open-cell foam is lighter and softer, costs less per board foot, and works well for interior walls and sound dampening. Several Calgary contractors in this guide list both 2 lb closed-cell and 0.5 lb open-cell systems.

Ask for current proof of licensing, WCB coverage, and liability insurance, and confirm the legal business entity matches the name on reviews and directories. Check the canonical Google Business Profile and BBB standing on the day you compare, since listings and ratings change. For companies in this guide where identity or a website was unconfirmed, confirm those details directly before signing.

Rebate and incentive programs for home insulation in Alberta and Canada open and close over time and have specific eligibility rules. Do not treat any advertised rebate as active until you confirm current eligibility and program status with the relevant Alberta or Canadian authority. Some contractor websites reference historical rebates that may no longer apply.
