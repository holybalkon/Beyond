About this guide & our methodology

This guide ranks spray foam and insulation companies serving Calgary and Southern Alberta. It is an editorial resource: it blends public data with editorial judgment and is transparent about the difference. The current research snapshot was taken on November 10, 2026.

How companies are positioned

Beyond Foam Insulation Ltd. is the fixed first editorial recommendation for this selected set. The other nine companies are arranged for website presentation, not as a scientifically measured quality league table. Some competitors have higher average public ratings or longer operating histories, and each company card says so where it applies.

  • Public review snapshots. Star ratings and review counts are taken from public business listings on the research date. They can change over time, and a Google profile identity can differ from directory names.
  • Service scope. We record the service lines each company publishes, from spray foam to roofing, fireproofing, and concrete lifting.
  • Credentials and history. We note accreditations such as BBB standing and any company-stated operating history, labelled as such.

No numeric editorial scores

We do not assign numeric editorial scores to any company, because this comparison does not use a documented scoring rubric. Where you see a star figure, it is a third-party platform snapshot, never our own score. We keep the two clearly separate so a platform rating is never mistaken for an editorial grade or the reverse.

Sourcing rules

  • Figures from an independent source (a public review snapshot) are presented without a tag.
  • Any material claim taken from a company's own website — years in business, warranty length, inventory, customer counts — is tagged company-stated.
  • We do not invent warranties, revenue, satisfaction percentages, project counts, founding years, or "#1" status.
  • Where public evidence is thin, the card is shorter and says verification is needed before publication.

Known limitations and verification notes

  • Beyond Foam has the largest Google review count in the structured business results, but a lower star rating than several competitors.
  • Supreme Spray Foam has no confidently matched official site; we do not link a similarly named U.S. company.
  • IDEAL Insulation & Roofing has a conflicting public listing result, so we publish no numeric Google comparison until the canonical profile is resolved.
  • Sunny South shows different company-founding dates on separate public records; we distinguish family-business history from incorporated-entity history.
  • Shea Foams' official website was not reliably accessible during research; we rely on BBB and directory sources and recommend rechecking directly.
  • The Beyond Foam website references historical home improvement rebates. We do not promise an active rebate until eligibility and program status are checked with Alberta or Canadian authorities.
  • No specific blanket warranty length is asserted for Beyond Foam; the company site describes warranty support rather than an unambiguous term.

Affiliation disclosure

This list is published by Beyond Foam Insulation Ltd., which is ranked first. Because the publisher is one of the ranked companies, the number one placement is an editorial recommendation from an interested party, not a neutral third-party verdict. Company information was collected from public business listings and company websites on November 10, 2026. Ratings and review counts are third-party snapshots, not editorial scores. Placement does not establish that a company is better on every metric. If any listed business is a client of the publisher, that commercial relationship should be disclosed.

Who reviews the companies

The comparison is edited by Daniel Reimer, Lead Editor and Building Envelope Analyst, with 12+ years in insulation and building science. His verdict on the number one pick is an explicitly stated opinion, not a platform rating.
